What a listing is — and isn't

Insurer recognition, CQC and professional registration details are shown as you enter them and labelled self-declared. Veyn doesn't verify them, so please keep them accurate and up to date. Members can report a listing that looks wrong, and we'll review it. Listings aren't shown to patients or the public. Veyn is a professional directory, not a referral agency, and doesn't recommend or endorse any provider.
