For consultants & clinics

Hiring

Hiring a private medical secretary, covered directly: cost, systems, coverage, and how contact works on Veyn.

C 01How do I hire a private medical secretary in the UK through Veyn?+

Search Veyn's directory of UK private medical secretaries, filter by specialty and system experience, and review skills badges earned through tested assessments rather than self-reported claims. Message a secretary directly using a per-profile unlock (£12) or a time-based pass. There's no agency fee and no percentage taken from any arrangement you make.

C 02How much does a virtual medical secretary cost in the UK?+

Day rates vary by region, specialty depth, and whether the work is remote or in-person. Veyn doesn't fix secretaries' rates — each one sets and displays their own on their profile, benchmarked with our day-rate calculator. What you pay Veyn is separate: £12 per contact unlock, or a pass for unlimited unlocks over a set period.

C 03Can I find a remote medical secretary for consultants?+

Yes. Secretaries state their working preference, including remote-only, and overseas-based secretaries list their time zone and which UK working hours they overlap — so you can filter for genuine availability, not just a job-title match.

C 04Do you offer locum medical secretary cover?+

Secretaries can set an "available now" status with free-text detail, searchable for short-notice or locum cover. At this stage that's filtered search, not an automated cover-matching system — worth knowing before you rely on it for an urgent gap.

C 05Can I find secretaries with Healthcode billing and invoicing experience?+

Yes. Healthcode and other billing systems are searchable fields on every profile, and proficiency can be backed by a passed skills test rather than a self-reported claim alone.

C 06Are secretaries experienced with Carebit, MidexPRO or DGL?+

Practice management and dictation systems — including Carebit, MidexPRO and DGL — are listed and filterable on secretary profiles, so you can search specifically for the tools your practice already runs on.

C 07What about consultant diary and clinic booking management support?+

Many secretaries list diary and clinic booking management among their previous roles, and it's filterable. A fully calendar-synced view is on our roadmap but not yet built — today, availability is a status and free text, not a live sync.

C 08Is Veyn CQC compliant, or does it check practice admin compliance?+

Veyn doesn't audit CQC compliance, and doesn't check ID, DBS, references or professional registrations for either side. Trust here is built on tested skills and genuine reviews, not stored documents. If CQC compliance matters to your engagement, that remains a check for you to make independently.

C 09Which areas of the UK does Veyn cover?+

Deepest in London and Milton Keynes, with particular strength in general surgery, orthopaedics and urology. Secretaries anywhere in the UK are welcome, but expect a thinner pool outside these regions or specialties while we grow — we'd rather say so than overclaim.

C 10What does it cost to browse and contact secretaries?+

Browsing and searching is free for registered consultant accounts. Contacting a secretary costs £12 per profile unlock, or a time-based pass (£75 / two weeks, £130 / month, £600 / six months). If an unlocked secretary doesn't reply within 14 days, the unlock is refunded automatically as platform credit.

For medical secretaries

Career

Whether you're moving from the NHS into private practice, already freelance, or upskilling — here's how Veyn supports the whole career, not just the next listing.

S 01What skills do I need to work as a private medical secretary?+

The core skills consultants look for are accurate medical terminology, confident audio-typing and dictation speed, and familiarity with practice systems such as Healthcode, Carebit, MidexPRO and DGL. Veyn's free skills tests turn each of these into a badge on your profile, so your ability is demonstrated, not just claimed.

S 02How do I move from NHS to private medical secretary work?+

Start by building a Veyn profile and completing the free skills tests. Our Business Hub covers the practical side of the move — self-employment and IR35 basics, a day-rate calculator, and invoice and contract templates — alongside Academy courses on the private-practice tools NHS roles don't typically use.

S 03What software do private medical secretaries use?+

Common systems in UK private practice include Healthcode for billing and insurer claims, and practice management or dictation platforms such as Carebit, MidexPRO and DGL. List your experience with each on your profile and validate it with a skills test.

S 04Are there medical secretary upskilling courses on Veyn?+

Yes. The core Academy curriculum — launch training courses and a monthly webinar — is free, permanently. Optional specialty packs (£15 each, or £35 for any three) go deeper into the clinical terminology and workflow of a single named specialty.

S 05Is Veyn free for medical secretaries?+

Yes. Your core profile, skills tests, community forum and the core Academy curriculum are free, permanently. Two optional extras exist if you want them — an £8/month visibility boost and one-time specialty packs — but neither is required to find work through Veyn.

S 06How do I find remote medical secretary roles or private consultant secretary opportunities?+

Build your profile, complete the skills tests, and set your availability status and travel radius — including "remote only" if that's your preference. Consultants search and contact secretaries directly, and you can opt in to job alerts so new matches come to you.

S 07Do I need ID checks, DBS or references to join?+

No. Veyn doesn't collect or require ID documents, DBS checks, references or registration numbers — that's a deliberate design choice, not a gap. Trust is built through tested skills and genuine reviews. If a consultant asks for your own DBS check, our guide covers where to get one.

S 08Does Veyn offer medical dictation and terminology training?+

Yes. The free skills tests include a medical terminology quiz and sample audio-typing dictations, each earning a badge on your profile once passed.
