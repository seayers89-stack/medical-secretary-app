Specialties we're deepest in, in London

Three specialties account for most of the searchable depth in London today.

General surgery

High-volume elective lists — hernia repair, cholecystectomy, colorectal referrals — with DGL and Carebit the most common systems.

Orthopaedics

Joint replacement, arthroscopy and sports injury clinics, with fast-turnaround insurer pre-authorisation on DGL and Healthcode.

Urology

Cystoscopy, PSA monitoring and robotic-assisted lists, typically on DGL or Semble.
